## 🧭 About Me

Hi, I’m **Xiong Xinchen** (`@xxc2007`), an undergraduate in Geography Science who builds solo with AI—turning raw ideas into durable, thoughtful websites.

Two threads connect everything I ship: **a changing world in space**, and **personal memory in time**. The first grew into a bilingual daily geography publication monitoring a thousand public sources; the second became a quiet digital chronicle keeping three years of red-brick halls, camphor trees, and an old water tower alive by Qingshan Lake. All three projects are open-sourced under **MIT**, hosted at `xxc2007.me`, and tended continuously.
