## 🧭 About Me

Hi, I’m **Xiong Xinchen** (`@xxc2007`), an undergraduate in Geography Science and an independent builder who works “solo, together with AI” to shape ideas into real, live websites.

Two threads run through everything I make: **the real world in space**, and **personal memory in time**. The first became a geography news site that publishes every morning at 08:00; the second became a permanent album that holds three years of my high school—its playground, camphor trees, and old water tower. All projects are open-sourced under the **MIT License**, live under the same domain `xxc2007.me`, and tended continuously by myself.

## 🛠 How I Build

1. **Clarify the purpose first** — Before writing the first line of code, state plainly what one job the page exists to do for its reader.
2. **Restrained, deliberate engineering** — Zero frameworks, zero build steps, and self-hosted assets for the static sites; TypeScript, PostgreSQL, and durable queues when a real content pipeline demands them.
3. **Cartography crafted by hand** — From NASA Blue Marble textures and multi-source MapLibre layers to spatial-salience rubrics, geography shapes every pixel.
4. **Ship it, then tend it** — Verification scripts, deployment baselines, and regression tests keep the three sites and the comment service coexisting cleanly over time.
